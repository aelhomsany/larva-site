Our product

Leaveo

Leave that adds up across every working week.

Leaveo is Larva's leave and vacation management product for distributed teams. Every charged day carries its reason: the dates counted, the dates skipped, and the workforce group policy that produced the result — visible before a balance changes, and still attached to the record after approval.

  • Different weekends and public holidays per workforce group, in one org.
  • Requests, approvals, balances and a team calendar in one place.
  • Free forever for up to five active people. No card.

How we work

Specification first, evidence at every gate.

Our delivery method is written down and applied to every change, whether it is a new epic or a one-line fix.

  1. Distil the intent into a spec

    Requirements are numbered and traceable. Nothing is built against a conversation; it is built against a document that can be reviewed and disagreed with.

  2. Write the failing test first

    Priority-zero acceptance criteria get a red-phase test before implementation starts, so "it works" means something specific rather than something hopeful.

  3. Review adversarially

    Independent review layers look for the failure the author did not imagine — data integrity, tenancy boundaries, migration safety, edge cases.

  4. Trace, then gate

    Every requirement is mapped to the tests that actually cover it. The gate reads that map, not a green checkmark on a dashboard.

  5. Release so it can be undone

    Expand-and-contract migrations, one-version compatibility between old and new processes, and a rollback path that does not depend on luck.
